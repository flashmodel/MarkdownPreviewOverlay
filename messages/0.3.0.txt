MarkdownPreviewOverlay v0.3.0
============================

- Inline Math: Renders inline LaTeX expressions in Markdown previews using
  `$...$`, `$$...$$`, and `\(...\)` delimiters. Common symbols, Greek letters,
  operators, fractions, roots, superscripts, subscripts, accents, and
  calligraphic or blackboard-bold notation are converted to readable Unicode
  with lightweight HTML styling where needed.
